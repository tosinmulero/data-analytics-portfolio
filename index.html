Data Science Flagship

Credit Risk Intelligence & Explainable Default Prediction

Production-style machine-learning case study covering model benchmarking, Optuna optimisation, probability calibration, cost-sensitive decisioning, SHAP explainability, subgroup auditing, MLflow tracking, FastAPI inference, Streamlit delivery and automated CI.

Key Results

  • 30,000 credit-card client records analysed.
  • Selected Optuna-tuned XGBoost after Logistic Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM and XGBoost benchmarking.
  • Final sealed-test ROC-AUC 0.7834 and PR-AUC 0.5622.
  • 80.71% recall at a frozen operating threshold of 0.145.
  • Illustrative validation cost reduction of 29.1% under a 5:1 false-negative:false-positive scenario.
  • SHAP explainability, subgroup audit, drift monitoring and GitHub Actions quality gates.
Responsible-use note: Portfolio demonstration using a public historical benchmark dataset. Not intended for real lending, underwriting or adverse-action decisions.

Python • XGBoost • LightGBM • scikit-learn • Optuna • SHAP • MLflow • FastAPI • Streamlit • Pytest • GitHub Actions

Customer 360 & Revenue Growth Analytics

End-to-end commercial analytics solution combining SQL, Python, Power BI, DAX, PBIP/PBIR/TMDL, customer segmentation, forecasting and experimentation.

Key Results

  • 270,154 customers analysed.
  • 4,419 purchasing customers and 775 repeat purchasing customers.
  • 362,165 GA4 sample e-commerce revenue.
  • Highest-revenue month: December 2020 — 160,555.

Data disclosure: Customer, acquisition, product, revenue and forecasting analysis use Google GA4 public sample e-commerce data. A/B experiment observations and marketing-spend examples are synthetic and explicitly labelled as synthetic.
