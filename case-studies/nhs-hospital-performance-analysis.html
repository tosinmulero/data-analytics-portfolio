NHS Hospital Performance & Patient Flow

Provider benchmarking, four-hour performance, 12-hour waits, regional variation and patient-flow analysis.

The problem

How can A&E performance be compared across providers without ignoring waiting-time definitions, provider scale or incomplete source submissions?

Data & provenance

Published A&E provider data prepared into 956 provider-month rows across 192 provider codes; 120 Type 1 providers feature in benchmarking.

Technical approach

  1. Validate and load hospital submissions in Python and PostgreSQL.
  2. Calculate four-hour performance and normalised twelve-hour waits in SQL.
  3. Compare national, regional and provider indicators and preserve data-quality flags.
  4. Publish the analytical measures in Power BI for provider benchmarking.

Documented findings

  • Analysed July 2026 A&E attendance reached 2,487,580.
  • National four-hour performance fell from 76.91% in April to 75.04% in August.
  • The lowest Type 1 performance quartile recorded 136.80 twelve-hour waits per 1,000 admissions versus 88.90 in the top quartile.

Decision relevance

Illustrates robust, transparent performance comparison and prioritisation of follow-up investigation.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Source figures can be revised; an incomplete July 2026 submission for East Kent Hospitals University NHS Foundation Trust is flagged. Associations do not establish causes.

Source & technical evidence

Follow the repository links to review analytical code, detailed methodology, dashboard captures and reproducibility documentation. Portfolio outcomes are not presented as verified external client results.
