Bellabeat Wellness Analysis

Fitbit activity and sleep analysis exploring behaviour, sedentary time, calories, sleep and user-level patterns.

Read the case study ↓Source repository ↗

The problem

What can a limited wearable-device dataset reveal about daily activity, sedentary behaviour and sleep for exploratory wellness-product questions?

Data & provenance

A small public Fitbit activity and sleep sample; activity and sleep datasets have different observation counts.

Technical approach

  1. Follow Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyse, Share and Act.
  2. Clean activity and sleep records with Python and pandas.
  3. Summarise steps, sedentary time, calories and recorded sleep.
  4. Build Power BI behaviour views and separate observation from recommendations.

Documented findings

  • Average valid daily steps are approximately 7,801.
  • Average sleep is 419 minutes, with reported sleep efficiency of 91.65%.
  • Mean sedentary time is approximately 1,083 minutes across analysed activity days.

Decision relevance

Gives a basis for further user research or optional wellness-engagement experiments, not medical conclusions.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Limited observational and non-representative sample. Not a clinical study, and no health causality or personalised advice can be inferred.

Source & technical evidence

Follow the repository links to review analytical code, detailed methodology, dashboard captures and reproducibility documentation. Portfolio outcomes are not presented as verified external client results.
