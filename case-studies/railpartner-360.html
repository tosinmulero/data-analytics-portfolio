RailPartner 360 — B2B Rail Retail & Customer Intelligence

End-to-end rail retail analytics platform combining PostgreSQL, dbt, Python and Power BI to analyse customer conversion, B2B partner performance, route reliability, operational disruption and refund-related commercial exposure.

The problem

How can rail retailers combine search funnels, partner sales, booking outcomes, route disruption and refunds into trustworthy commercial intelligence?

Data & provenance

Synthetic rail retail data covering 6 million searches, 2 million bookings, 250,000 customers, 50 B2B partners, 1,200 routes and 12 operators.

Technical approach

  1. Generate and validate synthetic records in Python and PostgreSQL.
  2. Build reporting-ready dimensions, facts and dbt transformations.
  3. Create consistent DAX measures and an auditable Power BI semantic model.
  4. Develop five executive, partner, customer, routes and refund reporting pages.

Documented findings

  • Synthetic search-to-booking conversion is 33.33%.
  • B2B accounts for 63.86% of bookings and simulated platform revenue is £9.03M.
  • Operational measures include an 11.23% disruption rate and £9.18M in refunds.

Decision relevance

The reporting model demonstrates how partner concentration, conversion leakage and operational risk can be evaluated together.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

All transaction-level results are synthetic, not proprietary Trainline or real rail operator performance.

Transaction-level rail data is synthetic and contains no proprietary Trainline or customer data.
