RailNexus — B2B Product Growth, Experimentation & Network Intelligence

End-to-end synthetic B2B rail product-intelligence platform combining partner growth analytics, rigorous experimentation, graph/geospatial opportunity analysis, predictive modelling, partner segmentation, responsible-AI investigation and production engineering.

The problem

How should a B2B rail product team connect partner growth, traveller friction, experiment evidence, network opportunity and booking propensity in one auditable analytical workflow?

Data & provenance

Deterministically generated B2B rail data: 600 partners, 10,000 sessions, 13,602 searches, 2,867 bookings and a 360-station network.

Technical approach

  1. Model partner activation, retention and funnel measures in DuckDB.
  2. Test Smart Journey Alternatives using session-level intent-to-treat analysis and experiment guardrails.
  3. Rank network and geospatial opportunity using traffic, route demand and friction signals.
  4. Benchmark leakage-aware predictive models and expose governed analysis through Streamlit and FastAPI.

Documented findings

  • Synthetic session booking conversion is 27.01%.
  • Experiment impact was −0.60 percentage points with p = 0.727, not evidence of a positive treatment effect.
  • The locked-test MLP recorded ROC-AUC 0.5632 and top-decile lift 1.257×.

Decision relevance

Shows how to turn diverse product evidence into prioritised questions and responsible experiments rather than equating model rankings with proven commercial impact.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

All partner, traveller, route and experiment data are synthetic. Opportunity scores are heuristics, not causal estimates or outcomes achieved for Trainline.

All partner, traveller, route, commercial and experiment values are synthetic. No proprietary Trainline or customer data is used. Experiment and opportunity outputs are not presented as actual Trainline business impact.

Source & technical evidence

Follow the repository links to review analytical code, detailed methodology, dashboard captures and reproducibility documentation. Portfolio outcomes are not presented as verified external client results.
