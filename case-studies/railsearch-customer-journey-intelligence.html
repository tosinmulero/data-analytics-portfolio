RailSearch — Customer Journey & Conversion Intelligence

Production-style digital rail product-intelligence platform covering search-to-book funnel analytics, zero-result and checkout-friction investigation, statistical incident analysis, anomaly detection, leakage-aware temporal conversion modelling, explainability and production scoring through FastAPI.

The problem

How can teams identify search-supply and checkout incidents, quantify funnel deterioration and separate observed anomalies from unproven causal explanations?

Data & provenance

Synthetic digital rail journey events: 260,724 searches and 77,759 bookings; no real customer or Trainline data.

Technical approach

  1. Build reusable search, checkout and booking analytics datasets.
  2. Measure zero-result rates, device friction and funnel conversion.
  3. Detect anomalies against time- and segment-aware baselines.
  4. Evaluate temporal conversion models and expose validated scoring via FastAPI.

Documented findings

  • Overall synthetic search-to-book conversion is 29.82%.
  • One simulated supply incident raised zero-result rates from 5.83% to 20.21%.
  • A simulated mobile checkout incident raised error rates from 5.69% to 20.66%.

Decision relevance

Supports investigation priorities such as route-level monitoring, checkout telemetry and incident escalation with clear uncertainty.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Synthetic behavioural and commercial values. Counterfactual revenue estimates are illustrative, not verified causal losses.

Synthetic customer-journey data is used throughout. No proprietary Trainline data is included. Commercial-impact values are simulated analytical estimates.

Follow the repository links to review analytical code, detailed methodology, dashboard captures and reproducibility documentation. Portfolio outcomes are not presented as verified external client results.
