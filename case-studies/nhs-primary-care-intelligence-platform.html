NHS Primary Care Data Management & Clinical Intelligence Platform

End-to-end, independently developed, entirely synthetic GP practice data platform demonstrating patient registration quality, simulated clinical correspondence and pathology review queues, governed SQL and dbt transformations, and six colourful Power BI reporting pages.

The problem

How can a fictional GP practice maintain consistent registration records, review administrative queues and report accurately without using real clinical records?

Data & provenance

Deterministic synthetic GP practice records: 8,000 fictional patients, 20,000 appointments and simulated correspondence, pathology and coding tasks.

Technical approach

  1. Generate fictional patient and operational records, then validate accepted inputs with Python.
  2. Organise PostgreSQL raw, staging, core and audit layers with SQL checks.
  3. Produce simulated administrative review queues and 12 dbt aggregate reporting marts.
  4. Present six Power BI pages spanning administration, coding quality, pathology queues and data governance.

Documented findings

  • Raw intake acceptance is reported as 97.68%.
  • The simulation contains 4,749 review cases and 3,240 outstanding tasks.
  • Six dashboard views support operational review rather than individual clinical decisions.

Decision relevance

A comparable real-world reporting design could help administrative teams find record-quality problems and triage backlogs, subject to appropriate governance and validation.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Entirely synthetic, independent demonstration. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHS; no real patient information, live clinical integrations, clinical advice or validated QOF outcomes.
