Olist E-Commerce Analytics

Commercial, customer, logistics and retention analytics with PostgreSQL, SQL, Power BI and RFM segmentation.

The problem

How do purchase concentration, delivery distance and service reliability relate to commercial performance and customer retention in an online marketplace?

Data & provenance

Public Olist Brazilian e-commerce marketplace data, including orders, customers, payments, shipping and review information.

Technical approach

  1. Join and validate marketplace entities in PostgreSQL.
  2. Define order-value, repeat-customer and delivery-reliability measures.
  3. Implement RFM-style segmentation suitable for a low-repeat-order dataset.
  4. Present sales, logistics and customer patterns through Power BI.

Documented findings

  • 96,211 delivered orders total R$15.37M; repeat purchasing is 3.00%.
  • Cross-state deliveries average 15.12 days versus 7.92 days within a state.
  • Late deliveries average 2.57/5 reviews, against 4.30/5 for on-time or early deliveries.

Decision relevance

Identifies delivery reliability and customer retention as useful candidates for further operational investigation.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Historical observational data; correlations between delivery timing and satisfaction do not prove causality.

Source & technical evidence

Follow the repository links to review analytical code, detailed methodology, dashboard captures and reproducibility documentation. Portfolio outcomes are not presented as verified external client results.
