Credit Risk Intelligence & Explainable Default Prediction

Production-style credit-risk workflow covering model benchmarking, Optuna tuning, probability calibration, cost-sensitive thresholding, SHAP explainability, subgroup auditing, MLflow tracking, FastAPI, Streamlit and CI.

The problem

What does a reproducible credit-default workflow require beyond predictive accuracy, including calibration, operational trade-offs and responsible model review?

Data & provenance

Public historical credit-risk benchmark data with a documented 6,000-observation final holdout.

Technical approach

  1. Benchmark candidate classifiers and tune XGBoost with Optuna.
  2. Review probability calibration and select a documented decision threshold.
  3. Report holdout discrimination, precision, recall and probability-quality measures.
  4. Apply SHAP for descriptive explanations and track subgroup auditing, MLflow runs and API assets.

Documented findings

  • Final holdout ROC-AUC is 0.7834 and PR-AUC is 0.5622.
  • At threshold 0.145, recall is 80.71% and precision is 34.91%.
  • The 29.1% modelled cost reduction is illustrative and dependent on assumed error costs.

Decision relevance

Makes model quality and decision thresholds reviewable, while preserving caveats before any consideration of real operational use.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Not suitable for real underwriting. Excluding protected attributes does not establish fairness; SHAP attributions are not causal explanations.

Portfolio demonstration using a public historical benchmark dataset; not intended for live underwriting decisions.
