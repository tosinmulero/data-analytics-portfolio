Customer 360 & Revenue Growth Analytics

End-to-end commercial analytics combining SQL, Python, Power BI, customer segmentation, forecasting and reproducible experimentation.

The problem

How can a commercial analytics team relate acquisition, conversion, retention, product mix and revenue trends while clearly separating public sample data from synthetic experiments?

Data & provenance

Public Google GA4 sample e-commerce data supports observed commerce analysis; A/B treatment and campaign spend data are synthetic.

Technical approach

  1. Prepare transaction, product and acquisition measures with SQL and Python.
  2. Define customer segments, cohorts, RFM groups and Power BI measures.
  3. Evaluate short-term revenue forecasting against holdout errors.
  4. Demonstrate two-proportion A/B inference and simulated marketing CPA/ROAS.

Documented findings

  • 270,154 customers are analysed, including 4,419 purchasers and 775 repeat purchasers.
  • Holdout forecasting reports MAE 1,809.72 and RMSE 2,278.03.
  • A synthetic A/B experiment reports 19.25% lift, not a real-world commercial result.

Decision relevance

Creates a decision-support framework for understanding retention, channel value and forecast uncertainty ahead of controlled business testing.

Limitations & responsible interpretation

Commerce metrics use a public sample; A/B and media-spend economics are labelled synthetic, not actual campaign outcomes.

Synthetic A/B and marketing-spend examples are explicitly labelled as synthetic in the project.

Source & technical evidence

Follow the repository links to review analytical code, detailed methodology, dashboard captures and reproducibility documentation. Portfolio outcomes are not presented as verified external client results.
